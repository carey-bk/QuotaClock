QuotaClock

Privacy, simply.

This website uses fictional accounts and illustrative quota values. Its demos run in your browser. They do not sign in to your AI accounts or change your Mac’s settings.

The website has no analytics, advertising cookies or third-party fonts. If GitHub release information is available, your browser may request public information from GitHub. Visiting or downloading from GitHub is subject to GitHub’s privacy policy.

The QuotaClock app connects to the providers you configure to read usage or balance information. The screen saver reads a local snapshot from the app. Provider credentials and account switching are handled by the native app, never by this website.
